THE PROJECT

About CampusPulse

Smart Campus Analytics: Predict, Optimize & Improve Student Success.

THE IDEA

Help campuses notice support needs sooner.

CampusPulse brings multiple student indicators into a single view so faculty and placement teams can understand patterns and coordinate timely support.

▤

Academic progress

Review CGPA, internal assessment trends and backlogs together.

◷

Engagement signals

Bring attendance, learning platform activity and assignment completion into the same view.

↗

Placement readiness

Identify where aptitude, coding or interview practice resources may help.

CampusPulse is currently a frontend prototype. All displayed records and statistics are fictional. It is not yet connected to college systems, CSV files or a live AI model.
